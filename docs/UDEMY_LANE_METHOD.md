# Udemy Lane Method

The Udemy lane converts a local beta course into a marketplace-course submission package.

It does not publish, upload videos, submit for review, collect payment, create testimonials, or claim revenue.

## Inputs

- `config.udemy_lane.json`
- `data/udemy_course_blueprint.json`

## Output

- `outputs/udemy_course_package/`

## Minimum local checks

- At least 30 planned minutes of video.
- At least 5 planned lectures.
- Course description over 200 words.
- AI-use disclosure present when AI helped draft or organize course material.
- No fake urgency.
- No fake testimonials.
- No earnings claims.
- No unsupported safety guarantees.
- No official endorsement claim.

## Revenue rule

Udemy revenue is not countable until a recorded course is accepted, an external buyer pays, and settlement evidence is logged. Draft packages, uploads in review, wishlists, carts, coupons, self-payments, and platform listing pages are not revenue.

## Next human/media work

The package is ready for recording. A human still must record the videos, check audio/video quality, create or approve a compliant course image, complete the Udemy instructor profile, and submit the course through Udemy's quality review process.
