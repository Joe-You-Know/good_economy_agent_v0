# Course Lane Method

The course lane lets the agent create an ethical education product from zero cash without requiring GitHub bounties.

## Core rule

A course is morally positive only when it increases learner capability, truthful perception, and future adaptive choice.

A course is morally negative when it relies on deception, fake scarcity, fake testimonials, unsupported claims, coercion, or information asymmetry.

## Valid course revenue

Course revenue is valid only if:

1. the buyer opted in,
2. the course claims were truthful,
3. the price was disclosed,
4. limitations were disclosed,
5. no fake urgency or fake testimonials were used,
6. no earnings claims were made without evidence,
7. the buyer received the promised content,
8. refunds/remedies were available as stated,
9. all fees and model/runtime costs are deducted.

## v0 boundary

Course Lane v0 creates local validation bundles only. It does not publish, message, collect payment, or claim revenue.
